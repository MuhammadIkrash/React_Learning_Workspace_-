Privacy

Exactly what CleanWeb Pro stores, in plain language.

What stays on this device

  • Your settings, whitelist, custom filters, and Zapper rules — stored in this browser's local extension storage.
  • Local statistics (counts of ads blocked, elements zapped) — never transmitted anywhere.
  • Playback position and speed/quality preferences, if you enable "remember playback position".

What CleanWeb Pro never does

  • Never reads your passwords or cookies.
  • Never collects or transmits your browsing history.
  • Never sends the URLs you visit to any server.
  • Never runs analytics or third-party trackers of its own.
  • Never injects remote JavaScript — every script shipped with CleanWeb Pro is in this package.
  • Never sells or shares data — there is no server to send it to.
